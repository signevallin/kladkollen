Getting started – fill your wardrobe

You can fill your wardrobe in three ways: import your garments from receipts, import from stores you've shopped at, or photograph a garment. When you take a photo, Skrud automatically fills in name, category, colour and season and removes the background. Choose whatever suits you – you can mix freely.

Photo tips – get the best results

Good photos help the AI recognise the garment correctly and remove the background cleanly: shoot in even daylight without flash, lay the garment on a smooth, solid-coloured surface that contrasts with its colour, smooth out creases so the whole shape shows, take one garment at a time straight from the front or above, and avoid coloured lighting that throws off the colour analysis.

Create outfits with AI

On the home screen you pick an occasion – Work, Casual or Party – and tap once. Skrud puts together a complete outfit from your own wardrobe. If you like, you can start from a favourite garment or a whole set.

Weather all day

Skrud factors in the whole day's forecast – temperature, changing conditions and chance of rain – and takes into account how quickly you get cold. If rain looks likely, the app reminds you to bring a rain jacket or umbrella.

Sets – garments that go together

Link garments that belong together, such as a suit or workout clothes. You can still style them separately, and with one tap build an outfit around the whole set.

Keep track of the laundry

Mark garments that are in the wash so they aren't suggested until they're clean. When everything is washed, you empty the laundry with one tap.

Order and overview

Filter your wardrobe by category, colour, size and season. Save garments you want to buy in the wishlist, mark items you want to sell in the sale list, and archive what you're not using right now – with a note on where it's stored.

Colour palette and capsule wardrobe

Run a colour analysis to get your personal palette with base, complementary and accent colours. With the capsule wardrobe you see your best staple pieces and how many outfits they can make.

A wardrobe for all of life

Start with your own wardrobe. With Skrud Premium you can share a wardrobe with your partner and gather the whole family's clothes in one place – the same app, wherever you are in life.

Skrud Premium

The core features are free. Skrud Premium unlocks unlimited AI outfits as well as a shared wardrobe for couples and families. The subscription renews automatically and can be cancelled at any time in the App Store.

Account and data

You own your data. You can delete your account and all your data at any time in the app under My profile → Delete account. Read more in our privacy policy.
